s1|Can't afford a hospital bill? Never assume you have to pay all of it.
s2|Many people get the bill, panic, and put it on a credit card.
s3|Now it is card debt with interest, and the hospital is done with you.
s4|Instead, ask the hospital for its financial assistance policy.
s5|Nonprofit hospitals have to have one. It can cut or forgive the bill if you qualify.
s6|Fill in the form, even if the bill is months old.
s7|Ask for financial assistance. Apply before you pay.
s8|Follow Red Pen for more ways to lower a hospital bill.
