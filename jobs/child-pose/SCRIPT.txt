s1|Child's pose feels tight? Never force your hips down to your heels.
s2|Your hips hang in the air, and you push them down anyway.
s3|Your knees and ankles complain, and the pose stops feeling restful.
s4|Instead, tuck a folded blanket between your hips and your heels.
s5|Or open your knees wide, so your belly can sink between them.
s6|Rest your forehead on a block, and let your arms relax.
s7|Support the pose. Then rest.
