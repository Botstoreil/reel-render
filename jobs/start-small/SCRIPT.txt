s1|First week of yoga? Never start with your deepest stretch.
s2|Cold muscles and a big stretch right away is a bad mix.
s3|You force your hands to the floor, and the back of your legs pulls hard.
s4|Instead, start small. Bend your knees and hang halfway.
s5|Breathe slowly, and let each breath take you a little lower.
s6|Stop where it feels like a stretch, not a pull.
s7|Start small. Go deeper slowly.
