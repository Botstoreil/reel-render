s1|Leaves on the road? Never brake or lean on them.
s2|Wet leaves are slippery. Brake on them, and the front wheel slides.
s3|Lean on them in a corner, and the bike goes down.
s4|Instead, look far ahead, and spot the leaves early.
s5|Slow down before them, while the road is still clean.
s6|Roll over them upright and smooth. No sudden moves.
s7|Brake before the leaves. Cross them upright.
