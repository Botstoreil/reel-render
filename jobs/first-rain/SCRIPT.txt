s1|First rain after a dry spell? Never ride like it's just a wet road.
s2|Oil and dust from the dry weeks come up, and the road turns slick.
s3|Brake hard or lean like normal, and the tyre slides out.
s4|Instead, slow down and leave a bigger gap.
s5|Brake early and gently, with the bike upright.
