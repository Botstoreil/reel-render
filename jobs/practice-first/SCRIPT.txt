s1|Adding something new to class? Never teach a pose you haven't practiced.
s2|You won't know where students tend to struggle.
s3|And your cues will come out vague and late.
s4|Practice it yourself for a week before you teach it.
s5|Write down what you feel at each step.
s6|Try it first on a friend or a small group.
s7|Practice first. Teach second.
s8|Save this for your next class. Download YogaFlow.
