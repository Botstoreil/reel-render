s1|Pigeon pose? Never let your hip hang in the air.
s2|You drop into pigeon, and one hip floats high off the mat.
s3|Your weight tips sideways, and the twist goes into your front knee.
s4|Instead, slide a block or a folded blanket under that hip.
s5|Flex your front foot, and keep your hips square to the front.
s6|Still too much? Lie on your back and cross your ankle over your knee.
s7|Support the hip. Protect the knee.
