s1|Low lunge on a hard floor? Never drop your weight onto a bare kneecap.
s2|The kneecap lands right on the hard floor, with nothing under it.
s3|It presses and aches, and you rush out of the pose.
s4|Instead, fold the end of your mat under the back knee.
s5|Or slide a folded blanket under it for extra padding.
s6|Set the knee a little behind your hip, so you rest just above the kneecap.
s7|Pad the knee. Then sink in.
