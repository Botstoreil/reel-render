s1|Teaching a one-sided pose? Never forget the second side.
s2|It's easy to lose track in the middle of a long flow.
s3|And students leave class feeling uneven.
s4|Write both sides into your class plan.
s5|Keep the same number of breaths on each side.
s6|Then say it out loud: same flow, second side.
s7|First side. Then second side.
