s1|Doing tree pose? Never press your foot into the side of your knee.
s2|Your knee bends forward and back, not sideways.
s3|Push on it from the side, and the joint takes a strain it isn't built for.
s4|Instead, place your foot on your calf, below the knee.
s5|Or higher, on your inner thigh, when your hips are ready.
s6|Press foot and leg into each other, and stand tall.
s7|Above the knee or below it. Never on it.
