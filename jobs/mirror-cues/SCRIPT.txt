s1|Facing your class? Never cue your own left and right.
s2|When you face them, your left is their right.
s3|And many students copy your body, not your words.
s4|Demo mirrored. Move your right arm when you say left arm.
s5|Cue by landmarks too: the arm closer to the window.
s6|Or turn sideways so everyone sees the same side.
s7|Their left. Not yours.
