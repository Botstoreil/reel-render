s1|Warm room, sweaty hands? Never ignore a slippery mat.
s2|In down dog, the hands start to slide forward.
s3|Students grip harder and the shoulders tense up.
s4|Keep a towel next to every mat.
s5|Dry the hands before down dog and plank.
s6|Or lay the towel on the top of the mat.
s7|Dry hands. Steady pose.
