s1|Checking a room for cameras? Never look only at eye level.
s2|Most people scan the walls straight ahead, and stop there.
s3|But a camera can hide high in a smoke detector, or low in a wall socket.
s4|So start high. The smoke detector and the corners of the ceiling.
s5|Then go low. Sockets, chargers, and anything near the floor.
s6|Finish at eye level, with the lights off and your flashlight on.
s7|Look high. Look low. Then straight ahead.
