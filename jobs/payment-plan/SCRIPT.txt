s1|Thinking of putting a hospital bill on a credit card? Never do that first.
s2|A card adds interest every month you carry the balance.
s3|And once the hospital is paid, it has no reason to negotiate.
s4|Instead, ask the billing office for a payment plan.
s5|Many hospitals offer plans with no interest. Ask what the monthly amount would be.
s6|Agree only to a payment you can keep up, and get the plan in writing.
s7|Ask for a payment plan. Skip the card.
s8|Save this for the next bill you get.
