s1|Running late? Never cut the final rest to save time.
s2|Rest at the end gives students a quiet moment to land.
s3|And skipping it sends everyone out the door in a rush.
s4|Plan the class so the last five minutes are already protected.
s5|If you run over, cut a pose from the middle instead.
s6|Give a clear cue to come back up slowly at the end.
