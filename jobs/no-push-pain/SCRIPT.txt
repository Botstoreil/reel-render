s1|Teaching a deep stretch? Never tell students to push through pain.
s2|Students will often push harder just to keep up with the room.
s3|Sharp, pinching or burning means back off, not go deeper.
s4|Cue a strong stretch, never a painful one.
s5|Tell them coming out a little is always allowed.
s6|Bring blocks and straps into the cue, not as a last resort.
s7|Strong is fine. Sharp means stop.
