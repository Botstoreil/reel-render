s1|Fast flow class? Never rush the transitions.
s2|Students jump from pose to pose and lose their breath.
s3|Feet land in the wrong place, and the next pose starts crooked.
s4|Cue the move step by step: step the right foot forward.
s5|Link each move to a breath: inhale, rise.
s6|Give one extra breath if the room needs it.
s7|Slow the move. Keep the flow.
