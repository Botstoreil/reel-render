s1|Wrists sore after plank? Never sink into the heel of your hand.
s2|When your weight drops into the wrist, all the pressure lands in one small spot.
s3|And with flat, closed fingers, there is nothing to share the load.
s4|Spread your fingers wide and stack your shoulders over your wrists.
s5|Press down through your knuckles and finger pads, like you are gripping the mat.
s6|Still sore? Drop your knees, or come onto your forearms.
s7|Spread your fingers. Press the knuckles.
