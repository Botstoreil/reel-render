s1|Playing music in class? Never let it drown out your cues.
s2|If students can't hear you, they watch their neighbor instead of you.
s3|And the back row usually hears the least.
s4|Before class, stand at the back and check the volume yourself.
s5|Pick songs without words for the parts where you talk the most.
s6|Turn it down for new poses, and back up once everyone is in.
s7|Cues first. Music second.
s8|Save this for your next class. Download YogaFlow.
