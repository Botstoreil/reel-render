s1|Lowering from plank? Never let your elbows flare out.
s2|When your elbows point out to the sides, your shoulders take the weight.
s3|The front of the shoulder gets squeezed, and the lowering gets shaky.
s4|Instead, hug your elbows in toward your ribs.
s5|Point them back, and stop halfway down.
s6|Too hard? Put your knees down first, and keep the elbows in.
s7|Elbows in. Point them back.
