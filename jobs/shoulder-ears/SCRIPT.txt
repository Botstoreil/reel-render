s1|In down dog? Never let your shoulders creep up to your ears.
s2|When they shrug up, your neck gets squeezed between your arms.
s3|And your wrists end up holding all your weight.
s4|Instead, spread your fingers and press the floor away.
s5|Draw your shoulders back toward your hips.
s6|Your neck grows long. Your head hangs between your arms.
s7|Press the floor away. Shoulders away from your ears.
