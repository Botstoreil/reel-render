s1|Teaching a class? Never open with the peak pose.
s2|Cold bodies can't reach the shape, so students force it.
s3|And a class that starts with a struggle is hard to turn around.
s4|Pick the peak pose first, then plan the class backward from it.
s5|Warm up the exact parts that pose needs, one step at a time.
s6|Offer an easier version, then the full pose for those who are ready.
s7|Build up. Then peak.
s8|Save this for your next class. Download YogaFlow.
