s1|New faces in class? Never start without a quick check-in.
s2|Some students carry an injury, a pregnancy or a sore back you can't see.
s3|And few will say it out loud in front of the room.
s4|Arrive early and greet each new student by name.
s5|Ask quietly if there is anything you should know about.
s6|Plan one easier option for them before the class begins.
s7|Ask early. Ask quietly.
