s1|Teaching a forward fold? Never let the knees lock.
s2|Students push the legs straight and lock the knees back.
s3|The pull lands in the back of the knee, not the hamstrings.
s4|Cue a small bend in the knees.
s5|Keep the weight over the middle of the feet.
s6|Let the chest fold down over the legs.
s7|Soft knees. Then fold.
