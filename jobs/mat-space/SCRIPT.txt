s1|Full class today? Never pack the mats edge to edge.
s2|In wide poses, arms and feet hit the neighbor.
s3|And students in the back row can't see you.
s4|Leave a hand's width of floor between the mats.
s5|Stagger the rows so every student can see the front.
s6|If the room is still full, pick poses that stay narrow.
s7|Give space. Give a view.
