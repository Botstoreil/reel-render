s1|Holding warrior two? Never let your front knee cave in.
s2|When the knee falls inward, it twists instead of tracking with the foot.
s3|It usually happens when your stance is too long, or you sink too deep.
s4|Stack your front knee right over your ankle.
s5|Point it toward your middle toes, and press into the outer edge of your foot.
s6|Can't hold that line? Shorten your stance. Less depth, better form.
s7|Knee over ankle. Toward the middle toes.
