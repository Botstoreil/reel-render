s1|In bridge pose? Never turn your head to look around.
s2|Your weight rests on your shoulders, and your neck is bent.
s3|Turn your head now, and you twist your neck under that load.
s4|Instead, keep your eyes on the ceiling.
s5|Press your feet and arms down. Lift your hips.
s6|Want to look around? Lower your hips first, then turn.
s7|Head still in bridge. Come down, then look.
