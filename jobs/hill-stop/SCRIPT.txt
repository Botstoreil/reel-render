s1|Stopped on a steep hill? Never hold the bike on the front brake.
s2|You hold the front lever, then let it go to twist the throttle.
s3|The bike rolls back, and you stall or drop it.
s4|Instead, hold the bike on the rear brake with your right foot.
s5|Your left foot stays down, and your right hand is free for the throttle.
s6|Find the clutch bite, add throttle, then ease off the rear brake.
