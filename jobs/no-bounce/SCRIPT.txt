s1|Trying to touch your toes? Never bounce to get lower.
s2|Bouncing makes the muscle tighten up to protect itself.
s3|So you end up pulling against a tight muscle.
s4|Go down slowly until you feel a gentle stretch.
s5|Hold still there and breathe for five slow breaths.
s6|Let each exhale take you a little deeper, only if it feels easy.
s7|No bouncing. Slow and still.
