s1|Teaching yoga? Ask before you touch a student.
s2|A sudden hands-on adjustment can startle a student.
s3|Some have injuries, pain or past trauma you cannot see.
s4|Instead, ask the class at the start, and let them say no quietly.
s5|Give a spoken cue first. Most students fix the pose by themselves.
s6|If you do touch, tell them where, and keep it light.
s7|Cue first. Touch only with a yes.
s8|Send this to a yoga teacher you know.
