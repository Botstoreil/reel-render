s1|Teaching a class? Never touch a student without asking first.
s2|A hands-on adjustment can feel very different to them than it looks to you.
s3|And many students won't say no in front of the room.
s4|Ask the whole class before you start, eyes closed, hands up for no touch.
s5|Ask again in the moment: may I adjust your hips?
s6|Offer a verbal cue first. Most fixes don't need a hand.
s7|Ask first. Every time.
