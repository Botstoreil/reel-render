s1|In a forward fold, bend your knees. Sounds wrong, but it helps.
s2|With straight legs and tight hamstrings, the lower back rounds to reach the floor.
s3|The pull lands in your back, not your legs.
s4|Instead, bend your knees until your belly rests on your thighs.
s5|Fold from the hips, then slowly straighten your legs a little.
s6|Stop when the back of your legs stretch, not your back.
s7|Bend first. Straighten slowly.
s8|Try it with YogaFlow. Download the free app.
