s1|Lying in final rest? Never sit straight up when it ends.
s2|Jolting up from flat on your back can leave you dizzy.
s3|And it yanks you out of the calm you just built.
s4|Instead, start small. Wiggle your fingers and your toes.
s5|Hug your knees, then roll slowly onto your right side.
s6|Rest there for a breath, then press up with your hands.
s7|Roll to the side. Come up slow.
