s1|Holding a deep pose? Never skip the way out.
s2|Students often drop out of it fast and all at once.
s3|And a sudden exit can strain knees, neck and back.
s4|Cue the way out as clearly as the way in.
s5|Use the same steps, in the reverse order.
s6|Give them a moment to pause before the next pose.
s7|In with care. Out with care.
