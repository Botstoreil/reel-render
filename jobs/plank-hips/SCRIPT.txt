s1|Holding plank? Never let your hips sag toward the mat.
s2|When the hips drop, your lower back arches like a hammock.
s3|The hold gets harder, and your back takes the load your core should carry.
s4|Instead, make one straight line from your head to your heels.
s5|Gently draw your belly in and press the floor away.
s6|Shaking already? Drop your knees and keep the line.
s7|One straight line. Knees down is fine.
