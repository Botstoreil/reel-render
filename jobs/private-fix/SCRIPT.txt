s1|See a student in a risky shape? Never call them out in front of everyone.
s2|Being singled out makes most people stiff and embarrassed.
s3|And a stiff body is harder to adjust.
s4|First give the cue to the whole room, without a name.
s5|If they still need help, walk over and say it quietly.
s6|Point to what is working before you change what isn't.
s7|Cue the room. Then whisper.
