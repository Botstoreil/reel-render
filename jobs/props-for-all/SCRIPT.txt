s1|Teaching a mixed class? Never offer a block only to the student who struggles.
s2|Singling one person out makes props feel like a punishment.
s3|So students skip the block, and strain to reach the floor.
s4|Ask everyone to grab blocks before class starts.
s5|Use a block yourself when you demo.
s6|Cue it as a choice for the whole room.
s7|Props for everyone. Every class.
