s1|No insurance and a big hospital bill? Never pay before you ask one question.
s2|The first bill often shows the hospital's full list prices.
s3|Pay that, and you may pay more than the hospital accepts from others.
s4|Instead, ask the billing office if there is a self-pay discount.
s5|Also ask for their posted cash price for your services.
s6|Get the new amount in writing before you pay.
s7|Ask for the self-pay price. Get it in writing.
s8|Save this for the next bill you get.
