s1|Tight back in a forward fold? Never lock your knees.
s2|Locked knees and a rounded back move the stretch into your low back.
s3|And forcing your hands to the floor only rounds you more.
s4|Bend your knees a little and hinge from your hips.
s5|Let your belly come toward your thighs, with your spine long.
s6|Rest your hands on blocks or your shins if the floor is far.
s7|Soft knees. Hinge from the hips.
