s1|Holding a hard pose? Never hold your breath with it.
s2|Holding your breath tightens your shoulders and your jaw.
s3|And you tire out much faster.
s4|Breathe slowly in and out through your nose.
s5|Let each exhale be a little longer than the inhale.
s6|Can't breathe easily? Ease out of the pose a little.
s7|Keep breathing. Ease off if you can't.
