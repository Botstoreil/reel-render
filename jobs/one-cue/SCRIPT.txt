s1|Cueing a new pose? Never stack five cues in one breath.
s2|Students can only follow one or two at a time.
s3|And the rest turn into noise while they wobble.
s4|Give the base first: feet, then legs.
s5|Let them settle for a breath before the next cue.
s6|Save the fine details for the second side.
s7|One cue. One breath.
