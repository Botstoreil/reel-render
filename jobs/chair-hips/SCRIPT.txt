s1|Chair pose hurts your knees? Never drop all your weight into them.
s2|When your hips stay high and your knees shoot forward, the knees take the load.
s3|And your heels start to lift off the mat.
s4|Sit your hips back, like reaching for a chair behind you.
s5|Keep your weight in your heels, so you could lift your toes.
s6|Knees stay over your feet, arms reach up beside your ears.
s7|Hips back. Weight in your heels.
