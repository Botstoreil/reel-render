s1|Teaching a new student? Send them this first.
s2|New students push into every pose to keep up with the class.
s3|They hold their breath and force the stretch until it hurts.
s4|Tell them they can rest in child's pose any time. No one minds.
s5|Breathe slowly. If the breath gets short, ease off.
s6|And tell the teacher about any injury before class starts.
s7|Your body sets the pace. Not the room.
s8|Send this to someone starting yoga this week.
